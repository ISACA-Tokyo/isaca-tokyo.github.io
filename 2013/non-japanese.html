SEMINAR TITLE Developing a Successful GRC Third Party Risk Management Program by Understanding Strategies and Industry Trends

DESCRIPTION Part I: Lecture

The results of the EY 2016 Financial Services Third Party Risk Management Survey and what we can learn from this about industry trends and how to develop a successful third party risk management program

Part II: Panel discussion

Discussion among corporate executives about third party risk management programs

SPEAKER MR. Harald deRopp, an Executive Director in the Financial Services Office Advisory at EY Japan for 14 years providing IT audit and IT risk management services specializing in governance risk and compliance and vendor risk management solutions.
